## What's new in v2.5.0
- **Tap a video → full screen.** Tapping an inline video opens the full-screen player in every viewing style (Cards, Relay, Full-Bleed, Magazine, Big Tiles, Streamline, Social; Compact/Apollo/Gallery thumbnails too).
- **Top time range.** Sorting by *Top* shows Hour / Today / Week / Month / Year / All time.
- **Hide post** and **Block subreddit** from the ⋮ quick-actions menu; manage blocked communities and unhide posts under *Content Filters*.
- **Recent searches** in the search dialog (tap to reuse, ✕ to remove, *Clear*).
- **Remembers your last feed and sort** when you reopen the app.

## Fixes in v2.5.0
- Blocking a subreddit now works even when the keyword/domain filter engine is off.
- A swipe starting at the screen edge switches feeds without also upvoting/saving the card under your finger.
- Account sheet: stats no longer squeeze, your avatar is shown, and Log Out asks for confirmation.
- User profiles: no fake "▲ 0 💬 0" counts; profile and Read Later lists fit short/landscape screens.
- Social view: no dead space under text-only posts.

## Also included (from v2.4.0)
All v2.4.0 UI, data and video-player fixes: dark launch theme and readable status bar, toolbar under the status bar, landscape-phone layout, endless load-more retry loop, RSS flair/body parsing, dropped session cookie, scroll-to-top in grid views, video gestures/audio focus/posters, autoplay setting, scrollable dialogs, and more.
